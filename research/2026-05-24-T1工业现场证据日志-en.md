## Pain Themes

- Last-meter verification gap: procedures require checking locks, gas readings, guards, PPE, hot-work clearance, or equipment state, but the field worker needs fast proof at the exact point of work.
- Invisible near-miss gap: forklift/person close calls, construction dropped objects, fall-protection non-use, abnormal alarms, and mobile equipment blind spots are rarely captured unless damage occurs.
- Alarm-to-action gap: predictive maintenance, SCADA/HMI alarms, solar inverter faults, and process abnormalities often stay in email/HMI screens without becoming prioritized work orders.
- Paper/proof gap: permits, JSA, LOTO logs, forklift checks, maintenance records, and agricultural equipment service are fragmented in paper, whiteboards, Excel, or chat photos.
- Legacy equipment gap: old machine tools, presses, pumps, farm equipment, compressed-air systems, and distributed energy assets lack built-in sensors and need cheap retrofit sensing.
- Temporary-work gap: hot work, confined-space entry, contractor repair, temporary construction zones, and loading/unloading are high risk because the hazard state changes faster than the management system.
- Energy waste visibility gap: compressed-air leaks, pumps running abnormally, and inverter/SCADA faults have direct ROI but are hard to localize and prioritize from raw readings.
